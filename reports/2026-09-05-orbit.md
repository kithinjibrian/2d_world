# EOD Report — 2026-09-05 (second report: orbit layer)

A second report for the same day. The first covers the units layer and the decisions that preceded
it; this one covers the orbit layer, which is a separate piece of work.

## What Shipped Today
- **The orbit layer.** Two-body integration under `F ∝ 1/r`, analytic screening path, apsidal
  measurement, insolation. 174 tests, `mypy --strict` and `ruff` clean.
- **Six consequences of the force law established in code**, not on paper: circular speed
  independent of radius, period linear in radius, apsidal regression of 105.4416°/orbit, season
  cycle of exactly `2+√2` orbits, a finite turning radius at every speed, and scale invariance.
- **Kell stubbed** and raising for everything beyond mass and luminosity.
- **`Quantity.scalar()` and `.array()`** added to the units layer, exposed by its first real
  consumer.

## What Went Wrong, And Was Caught
Two claims that survived a PRP review and a full implementation failed their first real test:
the boundedness demonstration proved nothing at high speed, and the stated reason for requiring a
symplectic integrator was wrong — a log potential is scale-invariant, so precession is insensitive
to energy drift while orbital *radius*, and therefore insolation, is not. A third error nearly
became a recorded finding: the first Euler mutation accidentally produced *symplectic* Euler.
