## What Shipped Today
- **The units layer — the project's first code.** 101 tests, `mypy --strict` clean, `ruff` clean.
  Dimension algebra with Fraction exponents, the 2D named-dimension table, `Quantity` for boundary
  checking, and the natural unit system where `G₂` and `σ₂` are both 1.
- **Mutation-tested it.** Substituting each 3D form in turn — inverse-square gravity, per-volume
  density, `T⁴` emission, per-area flux, per-area pressure, 3D viscosity — fails between 4 and 11
  tests. Every one is caught, and the suite is green again after restoring.
- **DECISION-015 resolved** — dimensions checked at module boundaries, not carried through kernels.
- **`PRPs/units-layer.md`** — the project's first PRP. Dimensional algebra derived and checked
  rather than recalled, yielding three discriminating tests (`G₂M/r` vs `G₂M/r²`; `2πRσ₂T³` vs
  `4πR²σ₂T⁴`; `ρgh` with 2D vs 3D density) and two findings: Reynolds number discriminates nothing
  and must not be used as a check, and 2D dynamic viscosity is `MT⁻¹` while kinematic viscosity is
  unchanged.
- **DECISION-009 resolved — scanned.** A world's dimensionless ratios are swept over a grid and
  habitability is an output rather than an assumption. Recorded in MEMORY.md decision 12.
- **DECISION-010 resolved — Kell stubbed** behind its real interface, with three binding conditions
  (tagged in the abstraction ledger, raises rather than defaulting, results reported as consequences
  of a chosen parameter). MEMORY.md decision 13.
- **Two architecture rules added** to CLAUDE.md, both forced by the scan: every layer runs at two
  fidelities, and scans are versioned data products.
- **A new anti-pattern**: a stub must never answer a question it cannot honestly answer, because a
  stub that returns a plausible default is never revisited and silently becomes the model.
